# Everyday ways to cope with stress

## Overview
There is no single right way to handle stress. Small, regular habits often make a bigger difference than one big change. Different strategies work for different people, so it can help to try a few and notice what feels useful.

## What you can try
1. **Move your body.** Even a short walk can lift mood and ease tension.
2. **Protect your sleep.** Aim for regular sleep and wake times.
3. **Eat regular meals** and drink enough water.
4. **Try a relaxing activity.** Breathing exercises, stretching, music or time outdoors are common choices.
5. **Set priorities.** Decide what truly needs doing now and what can wait. It is okay to say no to extra demands.
6. **Notice your thinking.** When thoughts become harsh or catastrophic, gently ask whether there is a more balanced way to see things.
7. **Stay connected.** Spend time with friends or family who make you feel supported.
8. **Limit things that add strain,** like excess caffeine or constant news and social media scrolling.

## Safety and limitations
These are general wellbeing habits. They support coping but do not treat any health condition, and they may not be enough on their own when stress is severe or long-lasting. Check with a doctor before starting new exercise if you have a health condition.

## When professional support may help
Consider speaking to a professional if stress lasts for several weeks, if you are relying on alcohol or other substances to cope, or if stress is affecting your health, studies, work or relationships.
