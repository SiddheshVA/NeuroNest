# Mental health support services in India

## Overview
In India, free mental health support is available by phone through Tele MANAS, the National Tele Mental Health Programme run by the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare. NIMHANS in Bengaluru serves as its nodal centre.

## Tele MANAS
- **Toll-free number:** 14416 (also reachable at 1-800-891-4416)
- **Availability:** 24 hours a day, 7 days a week
- **Languages:** callers can choose their preferred language; services are offered in about 20 languages
- **What it offers:** counselling by trained counsellors, with referral to mental health professionals such as psychologists or psychiatrists when needed
- **Who can call:** anyone with mental health concerns, or someone calling on behalf of another person
- A Tele MANAS mobile app is also available with self-help resources.

## Other places to start
- A family doctor or local government health centre
- College or workplace counselling services
- District Mental Health Programme services in your area

## In an emergency
If someone is in immediate danger, call the national emergency number **112** or go to the nearest hospital emergency department. The NeuroNest app handles urgent situations separately and will show current crisis contacts.

## Safety and limitations
Helpline details can change. This document lists general service information only and does not replace the app's crisis flow or official sources.

## When professional support may help
You can call a helpline or speak to a professional for any mental health concern, not only in emergencies. Reaching out early, when stress or low mood first starts to affect daily life, is encouraged.
