# Box breathing

## Overview
Box breathing is a simple, structured breathing pattern. It uses four equal steps, like the four sides of a square. Counting gives the mind something to focus on, which some people find helpful when thoughts are racing. It is a type of deep breathing exercise, one of the relaxation techniques NCCIH describes.

## How to do it
1. Breathe in slowly through your nose for a count of 4.
2. Hold your breath gently for a count of 4.
3. Breathe out slowly for a count of 4.
4. Hold again for a count of 4.
5. Repeat for 1-3 minutes.

You can trace a square in the air or on your knee with a finger as you go.

## Making it easier
- If holding for 4 feels uncomfortable, use a count of 3, or skip the holds and simply breathe in and out slowly.
- Keep the breath soft. It should never feel strained.

## Safety and limitations
This technique is not taken from a single official source page; it is a commonly used breathing pattern written by the NeuroNest team. Breath-holding may not suit everyone, including people with some heart, lung or pregnancy-related conditions. Stop if you feel dizzy, light-headed or more anxious, and return to normal breathing. It is not a medical treatment.

## When professional support may help
If you regularly feel breathless or panicky, or have a medical condition affecting your breathing, talk to a doctor. For ongoing stress or anxiety, a mental health professional can help.
