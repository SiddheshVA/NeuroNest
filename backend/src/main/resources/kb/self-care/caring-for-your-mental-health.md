# Caring for your mental health

## Overview
Mental health includes emotional, psychological and social wellbeing. It affects how we think, feel and act, how we handle stress, relate to others and make choices. Looking after mental health is important at every stage of life, not only when things go wrong.

Self-care means taking time to do things that help you live well and support both your physical and mental health. It can help with managing stress, lowering the risk of illness, and boosting energy. Even small acts of self-care in daily life can have an impact.

## Areas of self-care
- **Body:** regular movement, regular meals, enough water, enough sleep.
- **Mind:** relaxing activities, learning something new, limiting overload from news or social media.
- **Connection:** time with friends, family or community.
- **Purpose:** activities that feel meaningful, like helping others or creative hobbies.
- **Perspective:** noticing what is going well, setting realistic goals, practising gratitude.

## What you can try
Choose one or two small changes rather than overhauling everything. Notice what helps you, and keep doing that.

## Safety and limitations
Self-care supports wellbeing, but it is not a cure for mental health conditions. Some people need professional help, and that is not a failure of self-care.

## When professional support may help
Seek help if you have struggled for two weeks or more with things like sleep problems, appetite changes, trouble getting out of bed, difficulty concentrating, or losing interest in things you usually enjoy.
