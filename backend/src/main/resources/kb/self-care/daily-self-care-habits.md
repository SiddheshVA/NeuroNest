# Daily self-care habits

## Overview
Small, steady habits are often easier to keep than big changes. NIMH suggests a range of everyday self-care steps that support mental health. You do not need to do all of them; pick a few that fit your life.

## What you can try
1. **Get regular exercise.** Around 30 minutes of walking a day can help mood and health. Small amounts of movement also count.
2. **Eat regular, balanced meals and stay hydrated.** Steady meals and water support energy and focus. Cutting back on caffeine and sugary drinks may also help.
3. **Make sleep a priority.** Keep a regular schedule and reduce blue light from screens before bed.
4. **Try a relaxing activity.** Breathing exercises, meditation, journaling or a calming hobby.
5. **Set goals and priorities.** Decide what must be done now and what can wait. Learn to say no to new tasks when you feel overloaded, and notice what you have done rather than only what is left.
6. **Practise gratitude.** Note a few things you are grateful for each day.
7. **Focus on positivity.** Notice and gently challenge negative or unhelpful thoughts.
8. **Stay connected.** Reach out to friends or family who can offer emotional support and practical help.

## Safety and limitations
These habits support general wellbeing. They are not a treatment for any condition. Check with a doctor before big changes to diet or exercise if you have a health condition.

## When professional support may help
If you have been struggling for two weeks or more, or if these habits are hard to keep because you feel too low or overwhelmed, consider reaching out to a health professional.
